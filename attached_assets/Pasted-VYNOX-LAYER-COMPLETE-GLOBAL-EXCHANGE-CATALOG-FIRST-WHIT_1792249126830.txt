## 1. Exchange Products

Support only TWO customer-facing exchange products:

**SWAP — Crypto ↔ Fiat**

Examples:
- USDT → EUR
- EUR → USDT
- BTC → USD
- USDT → KZT
- USDT → RUB
- USDT → TRY

Swap uses configured bank accounts, payment methods, manual pricing, fees and limits.

**CONVERT — Crypto ↔ Crypto**

Examples:
- BTC → USDT
- ETH → BTC
- USDT TRC20 → USDT ERC20, where supported.

Convert uses Quickex or other supported conversion providers.

Remove Buy and Sell from customer websites, widgets, menus and available plan modules. Preserve historical data without destructive deletion.

- Provider name and category

- Supported countries or regions

Use an explicit payment_method ↔ fiat_currency relationship that supports multiple currencies where appropriate.

## 7. Super Admin vs Customer Admin

SUPER ADMIN controls:
- Global fiat currencies
- Global payment methods
- Global crypto assets and networks
- Global provider availability
- RPC nodes and blockchain infrastructure
- Master credentials
- Subscription plans, add-ons and entitlements

CUSTOMER ADMIN controls:
- Selection of entitled currencies and payment methods
- Selection of entitled crypto assets and networks
- Tenant-specific pricing rules, rates, fees and limits
- Tenant wallet addresses
- Permitted provider integrations
- Exchange and website configuration

Customer Admin cannot modify global catalogs or access central RPC infrastructure.

Telegram Bot, Telegram Mini App, Quickex, WhiteBIT and other optional services must follow plan entitlements.

Enforce tenant isolation and permissions on both the API and Supabase RLS layers.
